# Changelog

🐸 Stay up to date with the latest improvements and fixes. 🐸

---

## **Version 1.2.0**

Released: 18 August 2025

New delete account flow as per Apple guidelines, somehow got missed in my first app store review request

**Features**

- Previous app review request got rejected due to directing users to email the support email address for account deletion
- The app now deletes accounts in-app and no longer redirects to sending emails

**Other Changes**

- Updated Privacy policy with more details about account deletion

---

## **Version 1.1.1**

Account management and small ui changes + bug fixes

**Features**

- Password Management: Added Forgot Password and Change Password features for easier account recovery and security
- Improved Sorting: Swipe-to-sort now more accurately attributes who paid
- Maps Update: Expense locations now open directly in Apple Maps for convenience

**UI & UX Enhancements**

- New frog assets and fun drag-card animations
- New App Logo: Fresh look to match our evolving design

**Bug fixes**

- Trip codes are now case-insensitive, and expense details are capitalized/uppercased to reduce matching errors
- Safer button interactions to prevent accidental multi-taps

**Other Changes**

- New automation demos and tutorial videos to showcase expenses being automatically imported into the app whenever you tap with apple pay

`Initial Release`

## **Version 1.0.0**

Released: 30 July 2025

The first public release of the app! 🎉 (Australia only for now)

This version includes the core experience for managing shared travel expenses.

**Features**

- Sign up and login with email
- Log and categorise expenses
- Create and manage trips with a travel partner
- Automatically import Apple Pay transactions into the app using Apple Shortcuts
- View daily totals per person
- Automatically fetch daily exchange rates in base currency only in AUD (for now)

**Other Changes**

- Edited and published app landing template [Landing Page](https://cccsso.github.io/travel-sort-expense-landing-page/)
- Created and published the app [Privacy Policy](/privacypolicy)
